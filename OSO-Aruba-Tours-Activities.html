Additional Information:

What to Wear:

Sneakers or hiking shoes with a firm hold are highly recommended, as the terrain is uneven and coral cliffs can be sharp. Flip-flops are not allowed for safety reasons.

What to Bring:

Sun protection is essential: bring sunscreen, sunglasses, and a hat or cap to stay comfortable during the jeep tour.

Health and Age Recommendations:

OSO jeep tour is not suitable for guests with joint or back problems, pregnant women, or small children. We recommend a minimum age of 6 years for participants.

Valuables:

Please leave valuables such as credit cards, passports, and expensive cameras in your accommodation. The open vehicle will be left unattended at times, especially when visiting remote sites. By following these guidelines, you’ll ensure a safe and enjoyable jeep adventure in Aruba!

Cancellation Policy:

For a full refund, cancel at least 24 hours before the scheduled departure time.
