Frequently Asked Questions

OSO ARUBA TOURS (FAQ)

Do we drive our own vehicle, or is there a guide?

All of OSO ARUBA TOURS have a driver/guide who drives the Jeep. When you book a tour, you will be a passenger in the Jeep and will not be driving a vehicle.

OSO ARUBA TOURS Hours of Operation

Our office is open from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., year-round. During the summer months, our office stays open until 8 p.m. Our customer service team can assist you by phone from 7 a.m. to 9 p.m.

Do I need a driver's license to participate in the tour?

A valid driver’s license for ages 25 years old and above is required to drive the Jeep during the tour. Otherwise, you can ride as a passenger while a guide or another licensed participant drives.

How long do the Jeep tours last?

OSO ARUBA TOURS tours range from 3 to 4 hours. Custom itineraries can also be adjusted to fit your schedule.

What should I bring on the Jeep tour?

OSO ARUBA TOURS recommends bringing sunscreen, a hat, comfortable clothing and shoes, sunglasses, a camera, water, and any personal items. If your tour includes beach stops, pack swimwear and towels.

Can I get a private tour?

Yes! OSO ARUBA TOURS offers private tours for parties of 4 or more guests. Parties of 2 or 3 guests may book a private tour, but they must pay for 4 guests. Private tours are offered “by the seat” or “by the whole Jeep.” Hotel pickup is available on private tours booked “by the whole jeep” only.
